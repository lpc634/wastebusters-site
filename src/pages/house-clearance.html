What we take from a house or flat

A house clearance can be one room or the whole place, and a flat is no different. This is the sort of thing that goes:

  • Sofas, beds, wardrobes, mattresses and other bulky furniture, carried out for you.
  • Washing machines, cookers and dishwashers.
  • Boxes, bags and general household rubbish.

Before anything goes, we'll check with you what's staying. Anything that can be donated is put aside rather than binned.

For a price, send a photo of each room. The form on this page takes up to five. If there's more to show, WhatsApp them to us.
