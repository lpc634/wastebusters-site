What we take from a house or flat

A house clearance can be one room or the whole place, and a flat is no different. If it fits in the van, we'll take it:

  • Sofas, beds, wardrobes, mattresses and other bulky furniture, carried out for you.
  • Washing machines, cookers and dishwashers. Not fridges or freezers.

Before anything goes, we'll check with you what's staying. Anything that can be donated is put aside rather than binned.

A photo gets you a more accurate price. The form on this page takes up to five, and you can send more on WhatsApp.

Garages and sheds

We clear garages and sheds too: boxes, old tools, bikes and whatever has been in there for years.
