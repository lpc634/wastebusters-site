Common questions

How do I get a price?

Send us a photo of what needs to go and your postcode, on WhatsApp or through the form on this page. We'll come back with a fixed price, usually the same day. No obligation.

How soon can you come?

Same day when we can. We're open 7 days a week, 7am to 7pm.

Do I have to load anything myself?

No. We load everything and sweep up after ourselves.

Are you a licensed waste carrier?

Yes. We're an upper tier registered waste carrier, Environment Agency licence CBDU638282. You can look us up on the public register.

Will I get paperwork?

Yes. Every job comes with a Waste Transfer Note, the legal record that your waste went to a licensed carrier.

Is there anything you can't take?

We can't take fridges or freezers, paint, chemicals, asbestos, gas bottles, car tyres and wheels, batteries or fluorescent tubes. Your local recycling centre can take many of these.

Do you take washing machines and cookers?

Yes, and dishwashers. Not fridges or freezers.
