Your rubbish, disposed of legally

If rubbish you paid someone to take is found fly-tipped, it can be traced back to you, and you can be fined for handing it to someone without a licence. So check who you hire.

We're an upper tier registered waste carrier, licence CBDU638282, and you can look us up on the Environment Agency's public register. Every job comes with a Waste Transfer Note: the legal record that your waste went to a licensed carrier.

What we can't take

Hazardous waste needs specialist disposal, so we can't take fridges or freezers, paint, chemicals, asbestos, gas bottles, car tyres and wheels, batteries or fluorescent tubes. Your local recycling centre can take many of these.
