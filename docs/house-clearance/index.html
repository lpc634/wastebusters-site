What we take from a house or flat

A house clearance can be one room or the whole place, and a flat is no different. This is the sort of thing that goes:

  • Sofas, beds, wardrobes, mattresses and other bulky furniture, carried out for you.
  • Washing machines, cookers and dishwashers.
  • Boxes, bags and general household rubbish.

Before anything goes, we'll check with you what's staying. Anything that can be donated is put aside rather than binned.

For a price, send a photo of each room. The form on this page takes up to five. If there's more to show, WhatsApp them to us.

What we can't take

Hazardous waste needs specialist disposal, so we can't take fridges or freezers, paint, chemicals, asbestos, gas bottles, car tyres and wheels, batteries or fluorescent tubes. Your local recycling centre can take many of these.

In a house or flat, that's usually the fridge, the freezer and any old tins of paint. Leave those where they are and we'll take the rest.

Licensed, with the paperwork

We're an upper tier registered waste carrier, licence CBDU638282, and every job comes with a Waste Transfer Note: the legal record that your waste went to a licensed carrier.
